Decentralized Cold Chain Vaccine Monitoring and Alerting Blockchain System

A permissioned Hyperledger Fabric network spanning two organisations — the Ministry of Health and KEMSA — records every sensor reading to a shared ledger, so vaccine temperature history is jointly held and auditable rather than self-reported by whoever holds the stock.

Heat and freeze breaches are handled differently by design: readings above +8 °C escalate sequentially through four tiers on WHO time windows, while readings below −0.5 °C broadcast to every role at once, because potency loss from freezing is irreversible within the hour. Readings buffered offline and replayed past the damage window route straight to mandatory quarantine.

Every quarantine decision writes to an immutable six-step chain of custody, and five roles — Facility Custodian through National EPI Manager — get scope-filtered real-time dashboards, with PII kept off-chain in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019. Verified against a live Fabric network: 13 chaincode functions on a real ledger, three escalation modes end to end, 87/87 backend tests passing.
